**The short version:** Morb doesn't collect your personal data. Your videos and recordings never leave your iPhone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. To improve the app, Morb sends anonymous usage statistics, such as "an export finished" or "the Pro screen was shown", which can't be used to identify you.

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Morb app for iPhone and Morb's websites. In this policy, “Morb”, “we” and “us” mean the developer of the Morb app.

## Your recordings

Videos you import into Morb, and everything Morb makes from them, are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. All audio processing, including Clean Up, Voice Mix and Pitch Fix, runs on the device. We never receive, see or store your recordings.

If you delete Morb, its projects are deleted with it. Videos you exported to Photos or shared elsewhere stay where you saved or sent them.

## Photos access

When you import a video, you choose it with Apple's photo picker, which gives Morb only the video you select. Morb asks for permission to add videos to your library so it can save your exports. It doesn't read the rest of your photo library.

## Voice engine download

Voice Mix and Pitch Fix need a voice engine that Morb downloads the first time you use them. Apple delivers it through the App Store, the same way it delivers app updates. No recording or personal information is sent as part of this download. Apple's handling of App Store downloads is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes all payments for Morb Pro. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Morb checks with Apple, on your device, whether you have an active subscription or a previous unlock.

Apple provides developers with sales reports that don't identify individual customers.

When you start, complete, cancel or restore a purchase, Morb sends an anonymous event recording that it happened, which plan it was for and its price, so we can see how many people subscribe. This event doesn't include your name, email, Apple Account or payment details.

## Information kept on your device

Morb stores a few things on your iPhone so the app works: your projects, your settings and saved My Sound presets, and a record of which free projects you've used. This information stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

If you answer "What are you using Morb for?", your answer is saved on your iPhone and is also attached to the anonymous usage statistics described below, so we can see which kinds of users find Morb useful.

## Usage statistics and crash reports

Morb uses TelemetryDeck, an analytics service run by TelemetryDeck GmbH in Germany, to count how the app is used. This tells us, for example, how many people finish an export or open the Pro screen, so we can see what to fix and improve.

**What is sent:** a short list of in-app events, such as "project created", "export finished", "Pro screen shown", "plan selected" and "purchase completed". Each event can include the app version, your iPhone model, your iOS version, your region and language settings, your answer to "What are you using Morb for?", and details of the event itself, such as whether an export was video or audio, or which plan was selected.

**What is never sent:** your videos, audio, recordings, file names, project names, sound settings, name, email, contacts or location.

**How it stays anonymous:** TelemetryDeck receives a random identifier for your copy of the app, not your Apple Account or any personal detail. TelemetryDeck hashes that identifier before storing it and doesn't store your IP address, so the statistics can't be traced back to you. The data isn't used for advertising, isn't combined with data from other companies and isn't used to track you across apps or websites.

If you opt in to sharing with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may also send us crash reports and basic usage statistics. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to help you, and we delete it when it's no longer needed for that.

## Our websites

This page is hosted on victoirappstudio.com, which doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running.

morbapp.com, Morb's own site, also doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising. Its hosting provider may keep the same kind of standard server logs. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it delivers them.

## Children

Morb isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold personal data about you, there's usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you've emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, email us and we'll delete it.

Because usage statistics are anonymous, we can't link them to you, so we can't look up, export or delete one person's statistics. To stop Morb sending them, delete the app.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how Morb handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
