## Get in touch

Email [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com). It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

## Questions

### How does the plan work?

You answer five short questions. Kit Pouch then builds a dated plan that steps your strength down first, then your count, until you reach zero. If you would rather stop at once, choose Stop now.

### Can I change my plan?

Yes. Move your next step sooner or later, stop now, or start the plan again.

### I had a pouch after my quit day. Do I start over?

No. Log it and carry on. Kit Pouch never wipes your progress for one slip.

### What does the craving help do?

It walks you through 90 seconds of guided breathing. When it ends, you note whether you got through the craving.

### How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

Your subscription is through Apple. To restore it, use Restore on the subscription screen. To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Erasing your data in Kit Pouch does not cancel it.

### Can I stop the usage counts?

Yes. Settings, then Your data, then Share anonymous usage counts. See the [privacy policy](/kit-pouch/privacy) for what they include.

## Help with quitting

Kit Pouch is a planning tool, not medical advice. It does not recommend a nicotine strength or dose, and it cannot diagnose anything. For free support:

- **UK:** [NHS Stop Smoking Services](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/quit-smoking/nhs-stop-smoking-services-help-you-quit/)
- **US:** [Build your quit plan at Smokefree.gov](https://smokefree.gov/build-your-quit-plan)
