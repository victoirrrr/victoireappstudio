## The short version

Kit has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. What you log stays on your iPhone. I make Kit, and I cannot see your logs.

Kit is made by Vijay Kumar, trading as victoir app studio. If you have a question about your data, email [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com).

## What Kit keeps on your iPhone

- The drinks you log: the time, the drink, how much, and the price if you add one.
- Your day notes and your morning check-ins.
- Your settings, such as your name, your intention, reminder times and the look you chose.

This is stored in Kit's own storage on your iPhone. Kit's widgets read the same storage so they can show your day.

## iCloud sync (off unless you turn it on)

If you turn on **Sync with iCloud** in Kit's settings, Kit keeps a copy of your logs in your private iCloud database, using Apple's CloudKit. Apple holds that copy under your Apple Account. I cannot read it. Turn sync off in Kit's settings at any time. To remove the copy already in iCloud, use your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.

## Apple Health (optional)

- **Reading:** if you allow it, Kit reads your sleep and resting heart rate to compare them with your drinks. This is read on your iPhone and Kit does not keep it.
- **Writing:** if you turn on Apple Health in Kit's settings, Kit adds the number of drinks you log to Health.

You can change both in the Health app at any time.

## Weather and location (optional)

If you allow it, Kit uses your approximate location to show the weather on the Today screen. Kit keeps the location in memory only and never stores it. To get the forecast, your approximate location is sent to Apple's WeatherKit, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Face ID

If you turn on the Face ID lock, iOS checks your face. Kit never receives face data, only a yes or no from iOS.

## Purchases and the free trial

Kit Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; Kit only learns whether you have Kit Pro. Kit keeps the date your free trial started in your iPhone's keychain, so the trial is not reset by deleting and reinstalling the app.

## What Kit does not do

- No analytics, usage tracking or crash reporting.
- No third-party code or services, only Apple's.
- No ads and no tracking across apps or websites.
- Nothing is sold or shared.

## Exporting your data

With Kit Pro you can export your logs and morning check-ins as CSV files. Kit creates the files and you choose where to send them.

## Deleting your data

- Delete a single entry in Journal: open the day, tap the entry, then choose Delete.
- Delete the app to remove everything Kit keeps on your iPhone, apart from the trial start date in the keychain.
- If you used iCloud sync, remove the iCloud copy in your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.

## Who Kit is for

Kit is meant for adults of legal drinking age. It is not a medical service.
