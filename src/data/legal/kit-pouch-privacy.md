## The short version

Kit Pouch has no account and no ads. Your plan and your logs stay on your iPhone. It uses two outside services: RevenueCat, to check your subscription, and TelemetryDeck, to count anonymous usage, which you can turn off.

Kit Pouch is made by Vijay Kumar, trading as victoir app studio. If you have a question about your data, email [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com).

## What Kit Pouch keeps on your iPhone

- Your answers from the start: the brand and strength you use, pouches a day, years using, why you want to quit, the pace you chose and, if you add it, what a can costs. Your region (US or UK) comes from your iPhone's settings.
- Your plan, its start date and any changes you make to it.
- Each pouch you log: the time, the strength and an optional tag.
- Each craving you log, and whether you got through it.
- Your settings, and the weekly note.

This is stored in Kit Pouch's own storage on your iPhone. Its widgets read the same storage. Kit Pouch does not use iCloud.

## The weekly note

The weekly note is written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model. Nothing about it leaves your phone.

## Subscriptions (Apple and RevenueCat)

You subscribe through Apple, and Apple handles the payment. Kit Pouch uses RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your purchase details from Apple with an anonymous ID. Kit Pouch never sends it your name, email or logs.

## Anonymous usage counts (TelemetryDeck)

To see which parts of the app help, Kit Pouch sends anonymous counts to TelemetryDeck, such as: setup started or finished, the plan or the subscription screen viewed, a trial started, a pouch or a slip logged, the craving help used, a plan step taken or changed. Some counts carry broad ranges, for example a strength band or a pouches-a-day range, never your exact numbers, brand, notes or dates.

TelemetryDeck uses a scrambled device ID, not your name, email or advertising ID, and does not track you across apps.

**Turn it off:** Settings, then Your data, then **Share anonymous usage counts**.

## Notifications

Reminders (a plan step day, or the day before a trial ends) are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent from a server.

## The share card

If you share your progress, Kit Pouch makes an image that you send yourself. It shows no name, brand, dates or logs.

## What Kit Pouch does not do

- No account and no sign-in.
- No ads and no tracking across apps or websites.
- Nothing is sold.

## Deleting your data

- **In the app:** Settings, then Your data, then **Erase everything and start over**. This removes your plan, pouch logs and cravings. It does not cancel your subscription.
- **Everything:** delete the app to remove all of it from your iPhone, including your settings and the weekly note.
- To cancel a subscription, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

## Who Kit Pouch is for

Kit Pouch is for adults aged 18 and over. It is a planning tool, not medical advice, and it does not recommend a nicotine strength or dose.
