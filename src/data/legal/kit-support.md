## Get in touch

Email [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com). It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing when something went wrong.

## Questions

### What counts as one drink?

A glass, a can or a shot is one. If you prefer, switch to standard units in Settings.

### Can I change a log?

Yes. In Journal, open the day, tap the entry, then choose Edit or Delete.

### Who can see my logs?

Only you. They stay on your iPhone. If you turn on Sync with iCloud, a copy also goes to your private iCloud, which only your Apple Account can open. See the [privacy policy](/kit/privacy).

### What does "No drinks" do?

It notes a day without drinks, so the day counts as one. A day with nothing logged stays "not logged", and Kit never guesses.

### What is free, and what is Kit Pro?

Logging and your whole history are always free. Kit Pro adds Insights, Mornings, sleep from Apple Health, the monthly letter, Customize Kit, the Face ID lock, CSV export and writing your drinks to Health. The free trial lasts 14 days from your first log.

### How do I restore or cancel Kit Pro?

Kit Pro is a subscription through Apple and belongs to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any iPhone signed in to the same account. To cancel, open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

### Does Kit work offline?

Yes. Logging and your history work without a connection. The weather needs one, and so does iCloud sync if you use it.

## If drinking feels like a problem

Kit is not a medical service. If you want to talk to someone, [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) lists free, confidential helplines in your country.
