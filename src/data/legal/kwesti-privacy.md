## The short version

- There is no account to make.
- Your kwests, photos, notes and place names are kept on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud. We can't see them.
- No ads, no tracking and no selling of data.
- If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

## Who we are

Kwesti is an iPhone app made by an independent developer based in India. In this policy, “Kwesti”, “we” and “us” mean the app and the person who makes it. If you have any question about your data, write to [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com).

## What the app keeps, and where

Kwesti does not have its own servers for your data and does not ask you to create an account. What you do in the app is stored on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage.

| What | Why | Where it's kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which kwests you lived, swapped or skipped, and when | To show your week, your prints and your Journey | On your iPhone and in your private iCloud |
| Photos you take in the app | To make your prints | On your iPhone and in your private iCloud |
| Lines you write on a print | To show on the print | On your iPhone and in your private iCloud |
| A place name, such as a park or neighbourhood | To label a print with where you were | On your iPhone and in your private iCloud |
| Your settings, such as theme, reminders and rest weeks | So the app works the way you set it | On your iPhone and in your private iCloud |

Your private iCloud storage belongs to your Apple account. We cannot open it, read it or download it.

## Camera

The app asks for camera access so you can take a photo while you're doing a kwest. It only uses the camera when you open it from a card. The app does not read your photo library, and photos taken in Kwesti are not added to your library unless you choose to save or share one.

## Location

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location at the moment you live a kwest, to label the print with a place name. To turn your position into a place name, the app uses Apple's location services, which send the approximate position to Apple. Kwesti keeps only the place name, never your coordinates, and does not track where you go. You can say no, or turn location off later in iPhone Settings, and everything else keeps working.

## Notifications

If you turn them on, Kwesti sends one reminder on Monday morning when your new three arrive, and an optional midweek reminder if you choose one. These are scheduled on your iPhone. We don't use a push notification service or a notification token.

## When you write to us

The “Write to me” option in Settings opens your own email app with a message to [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com). If you send it, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to read and reply to your message, and we don't add you to any mailing list.

## Sharing a print

When you tap Share on a print, the app makes an image of it and opens the iPhone share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you pick where it goes, and from then on that service's own privacy policy applies.

## Analytics, ads and tracking

Kwesti does not show ads, does not sell or rent data, and does not track you across other apps or websites. The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising tools. Apple may share basic, anonymous crash and usage statistics with us if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your iPhone settings. These do not identify you.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting the app removes what's stored on your iPhone. To remove what's in iCloud as well, go to iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then manage storage, find Kwesti and delete its data. Because we never hold a copy, we can't restore anything you delete.

## Children

Kwesti is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. As the app keeps no personal data on our side, there is nothing for us to hold about anyone.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the GDPR in Europe, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. Almost all of it is already in your hands on your iPhone and in your iCloud. For anything you've sent us by email, write to [hello@victoirappstudio.com](mailto:hello@victoirappstudio.com) and we'll help.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. Big changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

## Contact
